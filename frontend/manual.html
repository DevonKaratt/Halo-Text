HALO TEXT manual

HALO is a Windows background app. It reads visible screen text and places translated text over the same area. English OCR is built in; optional source-script OCR, translation, and speech packs are chosen by the user.

1. Start HALO

  1. Unzip the entire Windows download into a folder you can write to, such as Documents. Keep its files together.
  2. Double-click HaloText.exe. A small blue H appears on screen and in the system tray.
  3. Right-click H to open all controls. Drag H to move it; double-click H to select an area.

2. Choose and download a language

  1. Right-click H → Translation language → choose an output language.
  2. Choose Download offline model for selected language if an offline pack is available. Wait for the notification that it is installed and warmed. The download may take time and needs internet. HALO shows progress in the H controls; errors are recorded in runtime/state/model-download.log.
  3. Open Downloaded language models to see installed packs and remove ones you no longer need. All 22 Indian output languages share one pack; removing it affects all of them.

The Windows download includes built-in English OCR but no optional source OCR, translation, or speech packs. The shared Indian pack is about 850 MB and an interrupted download resumes from saved chunks. Offline model availability varies by language: the local Indian pack covers English to all 22 Indian output languages, including Malayalam; other listed languages may use the approved online route when no local pack exists. Online translation sends recognized text, never screenshots, to Google’s public translation endpoint. Bodo and Kashmiri output require the shared offline pack. If the online endpoint rate-limits translation, HALO keeps watching the area and retries after a short delay.

2a. Read captions in another input language

  1. Open Input OCR language packs in the H menu. Download the writing-system pack for your source text; for Russian, choose Cyrillic. English OCR needs no extra pack. The browse-by-language list shows which scripts have a pack.
  2. Choose the output language, such as English. Leave Input language on Automatic detection or select a source manually when short captions are misidentified. Select the caption area.
  3. HALO asks whether to Use online, Use installed offline pack, or Download offline pack for a newly detected non-English source. Online sends only recognized text to Google. Offline downloads a direct pair when the Argos catalogue provides one.
  4. To remove an OCR or offline translation pair, open Downloaded language models. The model can be downloaded again later.

OCR packs are grouped by writing system, not one file per spoken language. Detection of short captions is uncertain, especially for languages sharing a script. The current OCR catalogue does not cover every Indian or global writing system. An OCR pack must be installed before HALO can identify text in that script.

3. Translate screen text or video captions

  1. Press Ctrl+Alt+R and drag a rectangle around the text. A green border stays visible.
  2. HALO reads that area and puts translated text over it. With watching on, it checks the same rectangle after text changes or scrolling.
  3. For video, turn on Faster captions in H controls and select only the subtitle strip. Small areas are faster. The selection follows its application window on the same display when Follow selected window is enabled.

Use Ctrl+Alt+A to pause or resume, Ctrl+Alt+T to translate again, Ctrl+Alt+O to show the original text, and Ctrl+Alt+C to clear the overlay and border. Ctrl+Alt+Q exits.

4. Audio translation

In Downloaded language models, download English speech recognition separately. Right-click H → Audio translation and voice → choose System audio or Microphone. HALO recognizes English speech, translates it to the chosen language, and can speak it with an available voice. Online voices send translated text to Microsoft. Voice matching is approximate; it does not copy the speaker’s identity. Use Ctrl+Alt+S to start or stop audio.

5. New caption and audio controls

In the H menu, keep Keep last caption until replacement enabled to avoid a blank overlay while a new caption is processing. HALO reuses translations for complete sentences that have not changed. Choose Check speed on this computer to time OCR on your selected area and local translation when installed; it does not measure online translation or automatically enable GPU acceleration.

In Audio translation and voice, choose Subtitles and speech, Subtitles only, or Speech only. Set a subtitle display delay from 0 to 3 seconds if captions lead the video. Approximate voice labels group pitch ranges; they are not speaker identification.

Speed and limits

Installed offline models avoid network delay, but speed also depends on your CPU, caption size, OCR difficulty and sentence length. Running multiple OCR packs may be slower than English-only recognition; HALO prioritizes the previously detected script on subsequent captions. HALO skips OCR when a watched caption strip is unchanged. No fixed sub-second time is guaranteed.

If a download fails, check runtime/state/model-download.log. For startup errors, check runtime/state/startup.log. The app and its downloadable models need a writable folder. The included source is MIT licensed; third-party models and libraries retain their own licenses.
